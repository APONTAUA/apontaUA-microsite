The presentation identified the following tools and methodology:

- **Slack:** team discussions and communication with supervisors;
- **GitHub:** source code, version control, collaborative development and project history;
- **GitHub Projects:** task organization and progress tracking;
- **Scrum:** iterative development, sprints, regular team meetings and continuous feedback.

A detailed communication plan was not produced. Meeting frequency and scheduling, individual responsibilities, communication rules, follow-up procedures, decision management and minutes were not formally defined in an MS1 deliverable.
