## Academic materials

ApontaUA arose from the need to centralize and organize academic materials at the University of Aveiro, particularly in the DETI context. Notes, summaries and other study resources are spread across different platforms and sources.

This dispersion makes academic materials harder to discover, organize and reuse. The presentation framed a shared, organized source as the context for the project.
