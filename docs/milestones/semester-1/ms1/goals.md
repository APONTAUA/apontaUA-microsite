The presentation introduced three project goals.

## Centralized Academic Documents

A single organized platform for academic notes, summaries and study materials, structured by course, curricular unit and topic, and accessible across cohorts.

## Collaborative Content Management

Students and teachers should be able to contribute, edit and share academic documents. The presentation also proposed versioning and collaborative improvement to encourage reuse and reduce duplicated work.

## Content Validation & Discovery

The proposal includes mechanisms to review and validate contributed content, distinguish reviewed from unreviewed documents, and make materials easier to find through full-text search and related-content suggestions.

These are project goals, not features delivered in MS1.

## Preliminary tasks

The presentation listed these task areas:

- Requirements Analysis;
- Architecture Design;
- Prototype;
- Development;
- System Development;
- Testing & Validation.

The list was preliminary. A detailed breakdown into individual tasks, modules, owners and deliverables was not produced. No tasks are attributed to individual team members here.
