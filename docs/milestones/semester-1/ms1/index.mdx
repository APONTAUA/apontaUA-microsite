**Milestone 1 · 29 September 2026 · PEI Group 17**

## Overview

MS1 was the initial definition and framing phase of ApontaUA. The team presented the project context and problems, related work, a proposed solution, goals and expected results, along with preliminary tasks, a communication approach and a project calendar.

## Transparency

The presentation was the only artifact produced and submitted for MS1. Several planning and documentation elements expected for this milestone were not developed to the expected level of detail. The pages in this section distinguish what was presented from what was not completed; they do not present planned features or missing documents as completed work.
