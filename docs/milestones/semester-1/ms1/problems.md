The presentation identified three problems:

## Dispersed materials

Academic notes, summaries and study materials are scattered across different places, without a single organized source.

## Duplicated effort

Different student cohorts repeatedly recreate similar academic materials instead of reusing existing contributions.

## Lack of structure and validation

There is no common structure for organizing materials or integrated mechanism to distinguish reviewed content from content that has not been reviewed.
