The presentation compared two existing student material platforms. To avoid adding claims not present in the slides, they are identified here by the slide on which each appears.

## Platform shown on slide 5

**Strengths identified**

- Organized by academic year and subject;
- Provides notes, summaries, exercises, slides and other study materials;
- Brings materials from different courses together in one platform.

**Limitations identified**

- Content may be outdated;
- Some courses are no longer part of the current curriculum;
- Some materials are duplicated;
- Coverage is limited to Computer Engineering / Informatics.

## Platform shown on slide 6

**Strengths identified**

- More modern and structured interface;
- Provides more up-to-date academic materials;
- Allows content to be organized and filtered through categories;
- Combines academic materials with other student services.

**Limitations identified**

- Materials can be scattered across different categories within the same course;
- Academic content is mixed with other services, including a merchandise store, university championship standings and other student association services.

These are the comparisons included in the presentation. No other platforms or evaluations are added here.
