## Introduction

MS2 deepened the understanding of ApontaUA's requirements and established a proposed architectural baseline for future development. The work moved the project from the initial problem statement toward a more detailed account of user needs, existing approaches, functional expectations, domain concepts and technical direction.

:::caution[Status of this material]
The requirements and architecture below document analysis and design proposals from MS2. They do not mean that the described platform capabilities have been implemented. The functional and non-functional requirements are working drafts; priorities, policy details and MVP boundaries still require validation.
:::

## Objectives

- Understand student and teacher needs in more detail.
- Collect and analyse questionnaire responses.
- Review related solutions and research.
- Define proposed functional and non-functional requirements.
- Identify actors and their goals.
- Model system use cases and the domain.
- Propose the system architecture and record its decisions.
- Develop mockups to discuss the main interactions.

## Results

The MS2 materials supplied for this microsite include two questionnaires and their statistical analysis, a State of the Art report, functional and non-functional requirement drafts, a use-case diagram, a domain model, an Architecture Decision Report, Figma mockups and the MS2 presentation.

## Points to validate across source documents

The source materials are preserved as MS2 artefacts. These differences are recorded here rather than silently harmonised:

| Topic | What the supplied materials show | Validation needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Academic year | The elaboration guide cover refers to 2025–2026; the MS2 reports and presentation refer to 2026/2027. | Confirm the academic-year label for the guide and milestone. |
| Actor model | The use-case diagram has Student, Teacher and Administrator. The functional-requirements draft also defines a Course Unit Responsible Teacher with unit-scoped delegation responsibilities. | Confirm whether the responsible teacher is a distinct actor or a specialised Teacher role. |
| Academic validation | The diagram connects Teacher to academic validation. The presentation says the responsible course-unit lecturer defines criteria and makes the final decision, with peer feedback informing it. | Agree who may validate and how peer review relates to final approval. |
| Domain model | The supplied domain diagram models `Note`, `Version`, `Rating`, `Report` and `MaterialRelation`; the presentation contains a simplified conceptual model using `Material`, `MaterialVersion` and `Review`. | Confirm which model is the intended baseline before implementation. |
| NEI page review date | The State of the Art report says the public NEI page was reviewed on 11 October 2026; the presentation says 12 October 2026. | Confirm the date used in the final source record. |
