## Purpose

The MS2 guide recommends designing mockups early. The presentation describes the mockups as wireframes for discussion, not a final visual design or implemented screens. Prototyping helps make the proposed library, material detail and submission interactions concrete before implementation decisions are made.

## Status

The presentation labels the screens as illustrative wireframes and notes that values such as course, curricular-unit year and semester are examples. They should not be treated as final curriculum data or implemented behavior.
