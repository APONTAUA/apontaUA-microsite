## What was accomplished

During MS2, the team produced most of the planned deliverables: functional and non-functional requirement drafts, use cases, a domain model, a proposed architecture, a State of the Art review, statistical analysis of the questionnaires and interface mockups. The presentation brought these strands together as a more detailed proposal for ApontaUA.

## What went wrong

A substantial part of the documentation was produced with support from Artificial Intelligence without the level of critical review, technical discussion and collective validation that the work required. As a result, some documents had problems with coherence, alignment and the justification of decisions. The team was not consistently involved in analysing, discussing and consolidating the work together.

## Feedback received

The presentation received significant criticism from the course's lecturers, particularly about the product proposal and the consistency of the work. Following that feedback, the team was advised to restructure the project. This summary does not invent quotations or add details that were not provided.

## Lessons and responsibility

As project leader, Daniel Simbe recognizes his responsibility in this process. He should have encouraged stronger participation across the team, required more rigorous document review and ensured that decisions were properly discussed and validated before submission.

The team needs a more consistent shared commitment, clearer technical discussion and collective validation of its work. Using AI-supported drafting does not replace understanding, review or responsibility for the submitted material. The next phase must address these shortcomings directly, without assigning blame to individual team members.
