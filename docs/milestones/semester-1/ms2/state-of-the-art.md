## Related work

Academic materials are shared through learning-management systems, student communities, shared drives and educational repositories. These approaches have different purposes: some support teacher-managed course delivery, some enable community sharing, and others publish professionally authored learning resources.

The source report connects the proposal to research on learning-object repositories and open educational resources. Its central design concerns are useful metadata, discovery, visible freshness and trust signals, and review processes. An uploaded study document is not automatically an Open Educational Resource: the contributor must have the right to share it and its licence or terms must be clear.

## Existing solutions

### Resumos de Informática

A subject-focused repository reviewed by the team during MS1. The MS1 presentation identified organization by academic year and subject, with summaries, exercises, slides and other materials. The team observed outdated items, duplicate materials and curricular units no longer in the current curriculum. This is the team's scoped MS1 review, not a new audit of every current resource.

### NEI Apontamentos

The public notes page reviewed for the report offers filters for academic year, curricular year, subject, author and professor, with categories such as bibliography, slides, projects, practical guides, exercises, tests, exams, notebooks and summaries. Contributions were invited by email. The reviewed page's visible academic-year options ranged from 2013/14 to 2022/23. That does not prove each item is outdated. A per-material version history and explicit validation status were not evident in the reviewed public interface.

### Studocu

A broad student-contributed document platform. The cited help pages describe search filters and a document-screening process for uniqueness and platform criteria, with a manual-review request available after rejection. A uniqueness check does not establish academic correctness, currency or alignment to a particular course's teaching.

### Moodle

Primarily a Learning Management System. Its resource model includes files, folders, books, pages, media areas and URLs in course spaces. It supports teacher-managed course delivery; the report compares that core resource model and does not claim that Moodle cannot gain features through configuration or plugins.

### OpenStax

A provider of free, openly licensed textbooks with expert authorship, peer review, updates and supplementary instructor resources. Its editorial model is a useful reference for review and maintenance, although its focus is curated textbooks rather than local student-contributed lecture materials.

## Comparative analysis

“Not evident” means the reviewed source did not establish a capability; it does not prove the capability is unavailable.

| Solution | Main purpose and organization | Contribution or quality approach | Limitations observed or scope of comparison |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Resumos de Informática | Informatics study materials organized by year and subject | Materials available; workflow was not assessed during MS1 | MS1 review observed outdated items, duplicates and legacy curricular units; scope was Informatics |
| NEI Apontamentos | Informatics student community; filters include year, subject, author, professor and category | Contributions invited by email | Per-item version history and validation status were not evident on the reviewed page; old year options do not prove every resource is outdated |
| Studocu | Multi-institution student documents with institution, course, category, year and language filters | Direct uploads, uniqueness screening and possible manual review | Uniqueness screening is not academic validation; the comparison is limited to cited public documentation |
| Moodle | Learning Management System with resources in course spaces | Teacher-managed course context | Not primarily a shared, cross-cohort repository in the reviewed core resource model; additional features may be configured |
| OpenStax | Curated educational textbooks | Expert authorship, peer review and updates | Not focused on local student notes and contributions |
| ApontaUA (planned) | University of Aveiro hierarchy: course → curricular unit → topic → material | Student and teacher contributions; planned duplicate warnings, version history and explicit review status | These capabilities are proposed and are not evidence of an implemented platform |

## ApontaUA positioning

The report does not claim that ApontaUA is the only platform with academic sharing, uploads, duplicate screening or review. Its proposed contribution is to combine a University of Aveiro academic structure with community contribution, a traceable material lifecycle, duplicate awareness, discovery and visible academic review. The proposal's value depends on the quality of implementation, licensing and provenance rules, and clear validation governance.
