## Sample and interpretation

The supplied report analyses **52 student responses** and **6 teacher responses**. These figures describe respondents only; they are not representative estimates for the University of Aveiro. Student results include multiple-choice questions where people could select more than one answer.

The report also states that 44/52 respondents (84.6%) rated the importance of knowing when material was created or updated as “Important” or “Very Important”. Eight responses were exported as “Option 3” and were not reinterpreted. For validation, 45/52 students (86.5%) rated identifying reviewed/validated materials 4 or 5 out of 5.

## Teacher findings

All **6/6 teachers** considered collaboration with students useful. The report also records that all six often encountered similar or duplicate materials; two answered “Yes” and four “Maybe” when asked whether a similarity-detection mechanism would be useful. Views on a dedicated aggregator were mixed: three rated its usefulness 5/5, two rated it 3/5 and one rated it 1/5.

## Limitations

- The student responses were concentrated in Informatics-related variants (47/52) and year 2 (46/52).
- The teacher sample has only six responses; proportions should be read with care.
- Multiple-selection responses are not exclusive choices.
- Some exported labels and open answers were not reinterpreted without evidence.
- The report presents descriptive statistics only; it reports no inferential tests and supports no causal claims.
