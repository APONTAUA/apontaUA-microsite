# ApontaUA — Product Concept

**Project:** ApontaUA  
**Institution:** University of Aveiro  
**Department:** Department of Electronics, Telecommunications and Informatics (DETI)  
**Academic Year:** 2026/2027

## Product Vision

ApontaUA gives the DETI academic community one place to find, organize and share study materials. It brings resources together by course and curricular unit, adds clear academic context, and lets students and teachers improve materials through constructive feedback.

The platform complements the University’s eLearning environment. It makes relevant resources easier to discover across academic years and supports community contributions while preserving authorship and material provenance.

## The Problem ApontaUA Solves

Academic resources are spread across course pages, shared drives, messaging channels and student communities. Their organization and context vary, so finding the right material can take time. Students may also repeat work without knowing that useful resources already exist, and have few structured ways to discuss or improve them.

ApontaUA addresses these issues with a shared academic structure, searchable materials, visible context and tools for collaboration.

## Users

- **Students** find and download materials, publish permitted resources, comment, rate contributions and suggest improvements.
- **Teachers** share and consult resources, provide feedback and participate in improving materials.
- **Designated student representatives** organize teaching resources from eLearning within the permissions assigned to them.
- **Administrators** manage accounts and roles, maintain the academic structure, review reports and oversee moderation.

Access and administrative actions follow role-based permissions. Relevant moderation and management actions are traceable.

## Academic Organization

Materials are organized through a consistent hierarchy:

**Course → Curricular Year → Semester → Curricular Unit → Material Category → Material**

Each material can show its author or source, curricular unit, academic year, type and publication or update date when that information is available. Teaching resources and community contributions share the same navigation while retaining their origin and applicable permissions.

## Core Capabilities

- **Find materials:** browse the academic structure, search by keywords and filter by unit, type, year or origin.
- **Contribute resources:** publish permitted materials with their author and academic context.
- **Improve contributions:** comment, rate, request revisions and suggest changes while respecting the author's control of their publication.
- **Track material history:** retain versions and relevant change information where version history is supported.
- **Stay informed:** receive relevant notifications about activity on a user's contributions.
- **Support trust and moderation:** report inappropriate content, review reports and take actions under established permissions and rules.
- **Reduce duplication:** identify identical files and flag potentially similar text so users can review it; complementary resources remain publishable.

Ratings and reputation help users understand community feedback. They do not certify academic correctness. Provenance identifies where material came from; it does not by itself establish permission to redistribute or scientific validation.

## Product Principles

1. **One consistent platform:** resources from different origins use the same academic navigation.
2. **Collaboration with clear ownership:** users can propose improvements without silently changing another author's work.
3. **Transparent context:** show authorship, source and academic context whenever known.
4. **Distributed responsibility:** permissions match assigned roles, with important actions traceable.
5. **Continuous improvement:** feedback and revision help materials remain useful over time.

## Initial Product Scope

The product direction includes University of Aveiro authentication, academic profiles, navigation by course and curricular unit, material discovery and publication, comments, ratings, revision suggestions, notifications, duplicate detection, reporting and moderation.

The Semester 1 MVP prioritizes the capabilities defined in the MS3 requirements and architecture. Search focuses on material title, description and metadata; OCR, text extraction and similarity analysis are planned as second-semester evolution. The detailed requirement documents define the approved behavior and boundaries for each capability.

## Summary

ApontaUA is a shared academic materials platform for the DETI community. It makes resources easier to find and understand, and supports students and teachers in sharing and improving them. Its organization, role-based permissions and visible provenance provide a clear foundation for useful and responsible collaboration.
