## Discussion Items

| Item | Who | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feedback from course instructors | Team members present | The team discussed feedback from the course instructors and agreed to use this opportunity to reconsider the project more broadly, while restructuring is still feasible. |
| Project restructuring | Team members present | The main focus was to revisit and restructure the work associated with Milestones 1 and 2. The discussion concerned the overall project direction, not only isolated changes to individual deliverables. |
| Teacher approval of submitted materials | Team members present | The team reconsidered the proposal that teachers should be the main people responsible for approving published materials. Questionnaire responses indicated that teachers did not want the additional workload of reviewing and approving a large volume of materials. The team identified the risk of creating a bottleneck if a responsible teacher was unavailable and could not delegate the task. |
| Shared and collaborative responsibility | Team members present | The team discussed the importance of avoiding overly centralized responsibilities, in line with ApontaUA's goal of being a collaborative platform for the University of Aveiro community. The material review and approval approach therefore needed to be reconsidered. |
| Milestone 3 | Team members present | Aspects related to MS3 were also discussed, although the main focus remained on restructuring the project and revisiting MS1 and MS2. |

## Other Notes & Information

- The meeting was prompted by feedback from the course instructors and took advantage of the fact that the project could still be restructured.
- One important point reconsidered was the expectation that teachers would be the main approvers of published materials. This approach raised concerns about workload, availability, delegation, and excessive centralization.
- The discussion focused mainly on restructuring the project as a whole, particularly the work associated with MS1 and MS2, while also touching on MS3.
