**Milestone 1 · 29 September 2026 · PEI Group 17**

The inception presentation introduces ApontaUA as a document management system for academic materials. It identifies dispersed resources, duplicated student effort, and the lack of structure and validation as the project's motivating problems.

## Research Group

The presentation identifies the team as **PEI Group 17**. The [team page](/apontaUA-microsite/team/) lists its members and supervisors.

## Context

The project concerns academic notes, summaries and study materials, with the aim of making them available through a shared, organized source.

## Problems

- Study materials are scattered across different locations.
- Different cohorts recreate similar academic materials.
- There is no common structure or integrated way to distinguish reviewed from unreviewed content.

## Related Work

The presentation compares existing student-oriented material platforms, noting strengths in organizing content and limitations such as outdated or duplicated materials and restricted course coverage. See the original slides for the examples and comparison details.

## Goals

The proposed goals are centralized academic documents, collaborative content management, content validation and discovery.

## Expected Results

The presentation lists requirements analysis, architecture design, a prototype, development, system development, testing and validation, with a working MVP for evaluation as an expected result.
