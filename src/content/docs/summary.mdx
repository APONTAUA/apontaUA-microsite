## Introduction

ApontaUA is a proposed collaborative academic document management system for the Department of Electronics, Telecommunications and Informatics (DETI) at the University of Aveiro. The project aims to make study materials easier to find, organize and improve.

## Problem

The MS1 presentation identifies three problems: academic notes and study materials are dispersed across different places; different cohorts repeat work by recreating similar resources; and there is no common structure or integrated way to distinguish reviewed from unreviewed content.

## Motivation

Students need a clear way to find relevant material from current and past cohorts. A shared academic structure can make existing resources easier to discover and reuse while giving the community a place to contribute and improve them.

## Goals

The MS1 goals are to support centralized academic documents, collaborative content management, content validation and discovery. The product concept expands on these goals with organization by course and curricular unit, useful material context, community feedback and transparent moderation.

## Proposed Solution

The proposed system centralizes academic notes, summaries and other study materials. Students and teachers can contribute, edit and share documents; versioning supports continued improvement; and review status and full-text search help users assess and discover resources.

## System Overview

The project concept describes a single platform organized around the academic hierarchy **Course → Curricular Year → Semester → Curricular Unit → Material Category → Material**. The proposal includes student and teacher contributions, roles for organizing teaching resources, and platform administration. The technical direction specified for the project is Python, FastAPI and PostgreSQL in a modular monolithic architecture. Detailed implementation documentation will be added when available.

## Project Scope

The scope is being elaborated during Semester 1. The MS2 documentation is organized around requirements, use cases, system architecture, a domain model, risk management, mockups and scope definition; some of these sections are still marked as work in progress. The [Product Concept](/apontaUA-microsite/product-concept/) contains the fuller initial proposal and open decisions.
